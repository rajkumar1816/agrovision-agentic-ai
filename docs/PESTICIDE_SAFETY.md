# Pesticide Safety

AgroVision does not generate pesticide instructions from the vision model. Chemical-control cards are populated only from verified Supabase knowledge records.

Every chemical section displays safety guidance:

- Follow the product label and use only legally approved products for the crop and target.
- Wear the protective equipment specified on the label.
- Do not exceed the label rate or mix products unless the label permits it.
- Observe the required pre-harvest interval.
- Keep pesticides away from children, animals, and food.
- Avoid spraying in unsuitable weather and dispose of containers according to local requirements.

When verified information is missing, no pesticide name, active ingredient, dosage, or waiting period is shown.
